Manufacturing Data Pipelines

I have developed and supported practical data flows that move information from manufacturing or equipment sources into analysis, reporting, alerts, and operational use.

Equipment → Engineering Analysis

Raw equipment files are extracted, cleaned, converted, and prepared for engineering analysis, plotting, or use in tools such as JMP.

Process Data → SPC → Action

Manufacturing measurements feed Statistical Process Control calculations, dashboards, alerts, and structured engineering response.

Environmental Data → Dashboard

Temperature and humidity data are collected automatically, appended to historical records, prepared for reporting, and used for threshold-based alerts.

MES Data → Local Manufacturing Hardware

Manufacturing information can be transferred through lightweight automation to local workstations or printers when direct integration is unavailable.

Integration Requirements & Technical Coordination

A large part of systems integration occurs before development begins. The right questions must be answered about what data exists, how it can be accessed, who owns it, and what the manufacturing process actually requires.

  • What information does the equipment generate?
  • What data needs to reach MES or another enterprise system?
  • What communication methods does the equipment support?
  • Is middleware required?
  • What data belongs in MES versus an external repository?
  • Where should large files or images be stored?
  • What licenses or vendor capabilities are required?
  • How will authentication and access be managed?
  • How will the interface be tested?
  • What happens when communication fails?

I frequently work between equipment vendors, manufacturing engineers, IT teams, MES teams, and leadership to help answer these questions before an integration is implemented.

Bridging Manufacturing, OT, IT & Data

From Raw Data to Manufacturing Action

Connected manufacturing is not simply about collecting more data. The objective is to create a reliable path from the physical process to the systems and people capable of acting on that information.

My systems work focuses on that complete path — equipment, connectivity, MES, databases, transformation, analytics, and operational use.
