Equipment Data Extraction & Engineering Analysis

Manufacturing equipment often produces raw files designed for the tool rather than for the engineer. Those files may be difficult to analyze directly, incompatible with standard analysis software, or require substantial manual preparation before the data becomes useful.

I have used Python to automate the translation from equipment output to engineering analysis.

  • Read equipment-generated files and extract relevant measurements and metadata
  • Clean inconsistent records and combine multiple files into structured datasets
  • Generate engineering plots directly from raw equipment output
  • Convert files into formats compatible with JMP statistical software
  • Prepare recurring equipment data for dashboards and engineering review

This allows process engineers to spend more time interpreting process behavior and less time manually restructuring files before analysis can begin.

MES & Manufacturing Workflow Automation

Python can serve as a lightweight bridge when MES, local equipment, printers, files, or other manufacturing resources do not communicate directly. These targeted utilities solve specific operational gaps without pretending to replace the underlying enterprise platform.

Barcode Workflow Example

One example involved an existing local printer that was not barcode-enabled or directly integrated with MES.   • Barcode-image and label generation
  • Email or message-triggered processing
  • Local workstation and printer interaction
  • Translation of manufacturing information into a usable physical output

Environmental Monitoring & Automated Data Pipelines

I developed automation that converted continuously generated temperature and humidity data into an operational monitoring workflow instead of relying on manual collection and review.

  • Scheduled extraction of temperature and relative-humidity data
  • Automated appending of new measurements to historical datasets
  • Data preparation for Power BI and SharePoint reporting
  • Threshold-based Microsoft Teams notifications for abnormal conditions
  • Historical trends and recurring dashboards for manufacturing visibility

This pipeline connected acquisition, preparation, visualization, and notification into a repeatable monitoring system.
