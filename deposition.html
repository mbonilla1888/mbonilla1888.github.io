Deposition Techniques

15+ years of experience across research and manufacturing-scale deposition processes.

  • Undergraduate: thermal evaporation for metal electrodes
  • Internships: graphene & CNT growth via CVD
  • Graduate research: MBE (monolayer VSe₂), PLD oxides
  • Industry: ALD, PECVD, e-beam, sputtering (optics & semiconductors)

Molecular Beam Epitaxy (MBE)

  • UHV PVD technique (10⁻⁹–10⁻¹¹ Torr)
  • Atomic / sub-monolayer control
  • Highest purity growth, low throughput
  • Not suited for mass production

E-beam Evaporation

  • PVD via electron-beam heating of source
  • Multi-pocket crucibles for manufacturing
  • Excellent thickness control (non-atomic)

Atomic Layer Deposition (ALD)

  • Self-limiting surface chemistry
  • Å-level thickness control
  • Highly conformal, manufacturing-capable

PECVD

  • Plasma-assisted CVD at lower temperatures
  • RF power controls stress & density
  • High deposition rates, cleanable chambers
