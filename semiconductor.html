Professional Semiconductor Manufacturing Experience

My semiconductor background combines direct industrial process engineering with broader manufacturing and technical leadership. I have worked in both commercial semiconductor manufacturing and defense microelectronics, supporting processes, equipment, production, engineering systems, and manufacturing improvement.

This experience allows me to approach semiconductor manufacturing from multiple levels: the physical process, the equipment producing it, the measurements used to control it, the engineering decisions required when conditions change, and the manufacturing systems used to execute production.

  • Semiconductor and microelectronics manufacturing
  • Process engineering and equipment ownership
  • Thin-film deposition and process development
  • Process qualification and manufacturing readiness
  • Metrology, process characterization, and data analysis
  • Statistical Process Control (SPC) and process monitoring
  • Quality, troubleshooting, and Root Cause and Corrective Action (RCCA)
  • MES implementation and manufacturing workflow development
  • Cross-functional engineering and production support

Intel — ALD & Semiconductor Process Engineering

At Intel, my work included semiconductor process engineering involving Atomic Layer Deposition (ALD) and direct support of manufacturing equipment and process performance.

The role required understanding the relationship between equipment condition, process behavior, manufacturing data, and production requirements in a highly controlled semiconductor environment.

  • Atomic Layer Deposition (ALD)
  • Semiconductor process engineering
  • Equipment and process ownership
  • Process monitoring and troubleshooting
  • Qualification and manufacturing readiness
  • Process data analysis and production support

L3Harris — Microelectronics Manufacturing
