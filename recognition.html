Lockheed Martin PMT Advocate Award

I received the Lockheed Martin Performance Management Team (PMT) Advocate Award for the fourth quarter of 2022.

The award recognized being a champion of continuous improvement and making a positive impact on manufacturing culture through the PMT process.

Lockheed Martin · 4th Quarter 2022

Performance Management Team Advocate Award

Award criteria included promoting a growth mindset through continuous improvement, actively participating in PMT, creating an inclusive environment, and driving positive manufacturing culture.

Candidates could be nominated by peers, PMT staff, and/or management.

My PMT work focused on creating visibility and accountability around operator concerns and manufacturing barriers and ensuring those issues were driven toward resolution.

Operator Engagement

Created structure for capturing concerns directly from production personnel so manufacturing barriers were visible rather than remaining informal shop-floor discussions.

Issue Tracking & Closure

Used dashboard-based tracking to assign ownership and drive issues involving processes, materials, equipment, safety, and workplace conditions through closure.

Continuous Improvement

Helped convert employee feedback into actionable improvement activities while maintaining visibility of open items, ownership, and progress.

Positive Manufacturing Culture

Supported recognition of employees who identified meaningful issues and encouraged participation in improving the manufacturing environment.
