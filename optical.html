Optical Coating Examples

Illustrations of common coating designs (AR/HR/bandpass/beam splitters/partial reflectors).

Intro

  • Design tools: FilmStar, OpenFilters, TFCalc, Essential Macleod
  • Methods: QWOT stacks and target-optimized thicknesses
  • Examples: V-coat, visible/IR bandpass, 4-wavelength bandpass, HR, beamsplitters, PR

V Coat

  • 2-layer V-coat design centered at selectable wavelength.
  • Used for narrowband applications (typically < ~40nm wide).

Visible AR Coating

  • 4-layer W-shaped design using oxides.

Wide Bandpass: Visible → Near IR

  • 12-layer design using oxides.
  • Reflection < 2%.

Wide Bandpass: IR ~3–5µm

  • 47-layer design using non-oxide materials.
  • Built from short-WL and long-WL bandpass designs; re-optimized to final stack.

4-Wavelength Bandpass (mixed indices)

  • 21-layer oxide design.
  • 9 transmission targets: blocked regions <10%, pass regions >95%.
  • Using material variants with different indices can improve passband performance.

Dielectric High Reflector (ripple reduction)

  • 20-layer oxide design.
  • Reflection > 99.9%.
  • Ripple reduction applied in tail regions.

Dielectric High Reflector (widen)

  • Widened by adding thicker stack + coupling layer.
  • Reflection > 99.9%.

Polarizing Beamsplitter

  • 24-layer design with reflection > 99.9%.
  • Reflects s-polarized and transmits p-polarized beams.

Non-Polarizing Beamsplitter

  • Splits incident light into specific reflected/transmitted ratios.

Partial Reflector

  • 2-layer and 5-layer designs with tunable reflection at 550nm (by material choice and stack).

Laser / Narrowband Partial Reflector

  • Reflection tunability achieved via number of layers.
  • R(14 layers) > R(12) > R(10).
