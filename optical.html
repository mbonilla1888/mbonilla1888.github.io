Optical Coating Examples

Textbook-style coating design examples created as illustrations (AR/HR/bandpass/beam splitters/partial reflectors).

Design tools & approach

  • Software: FilmStar, OpenFilters, TFCalc, Essential Macleod
  • Design methods: QWOT stacks and target-based optimization of physical thicknesses
  • Content below: examples spanning visible, NIR, and IR bands

4-wavelength bandpass filter (21-layer)

Placeholder for image: assets/img/optical/FourWL.png

  • 21-layer oxide design with multiple transmission targets.
  • Blocked regions: T < 10%; pass regions: T > 95%.
  • Using different index variants of the same material can improve passband performance.
