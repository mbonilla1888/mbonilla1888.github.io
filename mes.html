MES & Digital Transformation

I design and deploy manufacturing execution systems and operational data platforms that scale beyond a single line, single department, or single site.

My approach

  • Governance-first: naming standards, definitions, ownership, training, and auditability
  • Workflow clarity: shop-floor realities translated into simple, consistent digital steps
  • Metrics that matter: adoption, cycle time, tool availability, WIP flow, and actionable KPIs
  • Automation: eliminate manual status chasing and reporting via systems + alerts

What I build

  • MES configuration & data model: states, sub-states, transactions, attributes, genealogy
  • Dashboards: real-time visibility for tool/WIP/performance and weekly operational reviews
  • Operational workflows: standardized forms, approvals, escalation paths
  • Training: user enablement, documentation, and scalable onboarding

Leadership & scaling

The hardest part of MES isn’t the software but the alignment: standards, adoption, and making it usable across groups. My focus is building a structure that enables multi-team growth while maintaining quality and consistency.

  • Cross-functional coordination (engineering, ops, managers, stakeholders)
  • Roadmaps that prioritize stability + adoption before “features”

Typical architecture (example)

MES → database/analytics layer → dashboards/alerts → operational action.

  • MES transactions captured with standard definitions
  • Data stored/queried (SQL)
  • Dashboards and alerts drive action (Power BI / SharePoint / messaging)
  • Feedback loop improves process + quality
